# Music meaning + story alignment

This document records the emotional meaning used to place each soundtrack in the story. It intentionally does **not** reproduce copyrighted lyrics.

1. **Munbe Vaa** — longing, invitation, emotional closeness, and two people gradually feeling inseparable. Used for the opening invitation into the story.
2. **Vizhi Veekura** — being captivated by someone's look and presence, with playful attraction and a pull toward them. Used for the first waking/attraction beat.
3. **Enna Solla** — new love that is difficult to put into words, with feelings growing stronger than language. Used for the first conversation.
4. **Aedho Saigirai** — the mysterious inner change another person causes: emotional awakening, curiosity, and a new awareness of oneself through love. Used for getting closer.
5. **Idhazhin Oram** — open affection and the happiness of finally letting love be seen. Used when the story begins to feel like a real shared romance.
6. **Oh Penne** — yearning for closeness, companionship, and more time with the person you love. Used for the small-gift moment.
7. **Beautiful — short clip** — a tiny celebratory sparkle rather than a lyrical story beat. Used only as a quick surprise accent.
8. **En Kadhal Solla** — an intimate inability to hide love any longer; playful denial giving way to emotional surrender. Used for the story timeline.
9. **Yaar Indha Saalai Oram** — connection beginning through looks, silence, anticipation, and a moment that suddenly feels longer than it should. Used for the little-universe constellation scene.
10. **Yaaro En Nenjai Theendiyathu** — intense romantic fixation, finding the beloved reflected throughout the world and feeling their presence everywhere. Used for the photo stack.
11. **Oru Devathai** — enchantment and awe; the beloved makes ordinary time feel unreal and deeply beautiful. Used for the “Who you've become” chapter.
12. **Nee Partha Vizhigal** — the emotional power of one look becoming a lasting memory and returning again and again. Used for the birthday-card dedication.
13. **Ennodu Nee Irundhaal** — the transformative value of simple presence: if the beloved is beside you, life itself feels different and fuller. Used for the unsaid letter.
14. **Birthday Wish — little BGM** — a short celebratory birthday burst. Used for the balloon interaction rather than as a lyrical statement.
15. **Happy Birthday — little BGM** — another short celebration cue. Used for the cake/wish moment.
16. **Kadhaippoma** — emotional closeness through simply talking, sharing thoughts, and wanting the conversation to continue. Used for the questions chapter.
17. **Un Vizhigalil** — contentment in the beloved's presence, admiration, and the feeling that simply seeing them happy can be enough. Used for “Why I love you.”
18. **The Life of Ram — flute instrumental** — reflective spaciousness and memory without adding new lyrical meaning. Used for the museum of memories.
19. **Pachai Kiligal** — simple joy, warmth, and the idea that affection can make an ordinary world feel abundant and happy. Used for the tiny observations.
20. **Vaan Varuvaan** — anticipation and longing for the beloved's arrival/presence, with attention to all the little qualities that make them special. Used for the “100 tiny things” future-facing section.
21. **Maruvaarthai** — quiet vulnerability, reassurance, protection, and the ache of distance. Used for the slow “just breathe” pause.
22. **Anbil Avan** — enduring partnership and the choice to travel through different seasons of life together. Used for the “one thing” promise.
23. **Birthday Wish 🤍 Love BGM** — the exact uploaded 8-second birthday-love clip; used as the fireworks celebration cue. No lyric claim is made for the clip beyond its embedded title/metadata.
24. **Kaathalae Kaathalae** — love as completeness, companionship, and a feeling that fills everything you see. Used for the final emotional landing.

## Intentionally not used as a chapter track

- **Unakkenna Venum Sollu** remains in the public music folder as an available song asset, but is not forced into a chapter because its “let's go see the world together” theme would weaken the tighter emotional progression above.
- **Azhage Brammanidam** has been removed from the public music bundle after Chapter 23 was replaced by the exact uploaded birthday-love clip.
- **Azhagiye** is not present in the soundtrack.
